## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The reduced-motion rule only shortens durations to 0.01ms, which gives no cross-fade, and reveal-on-scroll items must not remain hidden when motion is reduced.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [x] #1 With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, all reveal-on-scroll items are fully visible without scrolling triggers
- [x] #2 Page transitions cross-fade instead of moving
- [x] #3 Verified with Playwright emulated reduced motion on /background and /writing
<!-- AC:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Reduced-motion: reveal targets forced visible in App.css; PageTransition cross-fades 150ms (opacity only). Verified with Playwright emulateMedia on /background and /writing.

Re-verified with emulated prefers-reduced-motion on a production build: no hidden reveal items on /about, /background, /writing, /talks or /contact. Out of scope: on the agentic-engineering article, interactive visuals deliberately start in a pending state under reduced motion (about 35 elements at opacity 0); article prose is visible.
<!-- SECTION:NOTES:END -->

## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Global reduced-motion rule in App.css now forces reveal-on-scroll targets (.about, .contact, .timeline, .timeline-item, .publications-events) visible, and PageTransition.css cross-fades opacity only. Verified with Playwright reduced-motion emulation: 0 hidden items on /background and /writing versus 8-9 hidden timeline items without it.
