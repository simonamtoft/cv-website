## The problem

The organisation wanted to know where AI was worth using. Before anything was built, the candidate use cases had to be identified, scoped and ranked against each other.

## What I did

I helped identify, scope and prioritise the use cases and turn them into a roadmap. I then built proofs of concept for two of them.

The first transcribes calls, summarises them, and tags each one by call type.

The second helps claims handlers work through document-heavy case files that otherwise require substantial manual handling and printing. It summarises the documents, builds a timeline of events and removes duplicates.
