## The problem

Every new product had to be placed in a category taxonomy as part of onboarding. That step stood between creating a product and getting it to market.

## What I built

I delivered hierarchical gradient-boosted models that classify products into the taxonomy, and integrated them into a large enterprise customer's onboarding workflow.

## Result

The models have been in production since January 2024 and expanded to multiple enterprise customers, reducing time-to-market.
