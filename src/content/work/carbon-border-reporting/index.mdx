## The problem

The EU's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) makes importers report the emissions embedded in the goods they bring into the EU. The evidence for those numbers sits with suppliers, spread over several tiers of a manufacturing supply chain, and it has to be collected, reviewed and verified every year.

## What I built

I built a bilingual demo platform for CBAM reporting. It orchestrates evidence collection, review and annual verification of embedded emissions across a multi-tier supplier network.

The platform models the regulation end to end. It starts with supplier responses and ends with certificate-cost exposure and a reporting dossier that can be exported.
