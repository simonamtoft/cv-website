## The problem

Getting a model from proof of concept to production took too long, and results were hard to reproduce from one team to the next.

## What I built

I architected a platform blueprint that standardises four things: feature management, the model registry, CI/CD, and Terraform-based cloud templates.

The aim was a shorter cycle from proof of concept to production and better reproducibility across teams.
